# Iconography Engineering

## Requirements

Prefer one coherent icon family unless a deliberate design reason exists.

For each important icon verify:
- semantic meaning,
- differentiation from neighboring actions,
- optical rather than merely numeric size,
- baseline and container alignment,
- stroke/fill weight consistency,
- clickable hit target,
- hover/focus/active/disabled treatment,
- contrast in light/dark themes,
- no broken glyph/fallback,
- no arbitrary emoji replacement,
- no icon whose metaphor is culturally/contextually confusing.

## Visual review

An icon can be technically correct and still feel wrong. Inspect its relationship to label, container, adjacent icons, visual weight, and surrounding density.
