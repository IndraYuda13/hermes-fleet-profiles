## Literature Review rubric - all four programs in the bundled forms

The TA1-05 forms shown for Informatika, Teknologi Informasi, Sains Data, and Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak use the same aspect weights:

| Aspect | Max |
|---|---:|
| Quality of literature as main references | 10 |
| Literature content related to problem formulation | 10 |
| Literature analysis related to objective/main idea | 10 |
| Completeness of theory study on method/algorithm | 10 |
| Strengths and weaknesses of prior research | 40 |
| Relation of theory to proposal topic | 20 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### Review strategy

Prioritize comparative analysis of previous work. The largest single component is strengths/weaknesses of prior research (40). A literature review that only defines terms can look complete but still be weak against this rubric.

## Guidance/Bimbingan rubric

The TA1-01B form shown for Informatika, Teknologi Informasi, Sains Data, and RPL follows this structure:

| Component | Criteria | Max |
|---|---|---:|
| Topic selection | Ability to select and justify the topic from background and problem formulation | 15 |
| Research question/problem statement | Ability to present the research question/problem statement to build problem formulation and objectives | 15 |
| Idea/strategy | Idea/strategy for solving the problem | 10 |
| Literature review | Justification of model/method/theory using literature | 10 |
| Implementation/simulation/computation plan | Explanation of how implementation/simulation/computation follows from the model | 10 |
| Independence | Student independence in preparing the proposal | 20 |
| Guidance process | Quality/participation in the guidance process | 20 |
| **Total** |  | **100** |

### Review strategy

Do not optimize only the written artifact. The rubric also rewards independence and the process of guidance. Help the user arrive at meetings with explicit questions, progress since the previous meeting, evidence reviewed, decisions made, and a next-step plan.

### DE audit method

When the user asks for a DE audit:

1. Select the correct program rubric.
2. For each aspect, cite concrete evidence from the user's draft.
3. Identify missing evidence, not just weak prose.
4. Give a provisional readiness score only if the user asks; label it as an assistant estimate, not an official grade.
5. Prioritize high-weight weaknesses first.
6. Check feasibility explicitly: time, data, compute/tools, access, and evaluation resources.

## Seminar preparation method

Build the spoken story in this order:

1. What concrete problem exists?
2. What evidence shows it matters?
3. What has prior work tried?
4. What remains unresolved?
5. What exact problem will this TA address?
6. What objective follows from that problem?
7. Why is the chosen method appropriate?
8. What data/system/context will be used?
9. How will the result be evaluated?
10. What result/contribution is expected without claiming unobserved results?

Then drill questions around the highest-risk assumptions and highest-weight rubric items.

## Relevant CLOs from the bundled guide

### S1 Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak

- CLO 2.2 / Sub-CLO 2.2.1: examine problems related to software as a proposed solution.
- CLO 3.3 / Sub-CLO 3.3.1: communicate and make appropriate decisions in solving problems based on analysis of information/data.
- CLO 3.3 / Sub-CLO 3.3.2: communicate proposed software-engineering solutions scientifically.
- CLO 6.1 / Sub-CLO 6.1.1: describe scientific principles to produce software-engineering-based solutions.

### S1 Informatika

CLO-07-2 emphasizes effective written/oral communication, studying IPTEK-related problems, making decisions from information/data, describing scientific principles for solutions, and communicating scientific solutions/ideas.

### S1 Teknologi Informasi

The bundled CLOs emphasize clear/comprehensive problem formulation and deep/relevant literature review, constructive guidance participation, effective seminar delivery, revision after DE, and research-problem formulation.

### S1 Sains Data

The bundled CLOs emphasize scientific solution principles, study of the problem domain/theory, decisions based on information/data analysis, and selection/communication of appropriate methods for data problems.
