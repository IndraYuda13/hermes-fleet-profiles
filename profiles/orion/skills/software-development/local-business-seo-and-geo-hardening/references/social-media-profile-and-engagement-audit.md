# Social Media Profile & Engagement Audit

Procedures for evaluating social media profiles (Instagram, TikTok, X) to diagnose reach problems, measure real audience engagement, audit content provenance, and build commercial conversion funnels.

## 1. Unauthenticated Profile & Content Extraction
When inspecting target or competitor accounts without logged-in browser sessions:
- Direct fetch requests to profile URLs on walled platforms fail with redirects to `/accounts/login/` or empty shells.
- Bypass via search engine indexing: query `web_search` using `site:instagram.com/<username>` or `"<username>" site:instagram.com`.
- Search indexing bots bypass login gates, returning cached bio, follower/following counts, recent post snippets, likes, and upload dates.

## 2. Engagement Rate (ER) Diagnosis & Benchmarks
Calculate the engagement baseline across the 10 most recent posts/reels:
$$\text{ER} = \frac{\text{Average Likes} + \text{Average Comments}}{\text{Total Followers}} \times 100\%$$
- Healthy Niche / Micro-Influencer (10k–100k followers): 1.5%–3.5%+.
- Severely Depressed / Ghost Audience (< 0.5%): 25k+ followers with only 10–50 likes indicates algorithmic throttling from passive consumption, purchased followers, or lack of conversational triggers.

## 3. Full-Funnel Profile Audit Matrix
- **Bio Copywriting Formula:** (1) Who/Value niche identity -> (2) Daily proof/cadence -> (3) Single high-intent link. Group biolinks into clear intent buckets (Curated Best-Sellers, Community Chat, Promo).
- **Middle-of-Funnel Conversion:** Avoid generic captions ("Strike mantap"); use debate hooks ("Menurut kalian ini salah joran atau teknik narik?") and comment keyword triggers ("Komen SPILL untuk info").
- **Story Nurturing:** Deploy 2–4 daily interactive stories with native stickers (Poll, Quiz, Slider) to activate Instagram graph algorithm timeline priority.
- **Bottom-of-Funnel Segmentation:** Migrate followers into owned channels (WhatsApp Groups, Telegram Channels, Email lists) where conversion rates are 5–10x higher.

## 4. Repost / Aggregator Brand Pivot (70/20/10 Framework)
For accounts built on third-party content facing algorithmic down-ranking or copyright strike risks:
1. **70% Transformative Curated Content:** Add voiceover analysis, tactical breakdowns, or educational commentary cards.
2. **20% User-Generated Content (UGC):** Solicit follower submissions via DM to secure explicit publication rights.
3. **10% Original Brand & Commercial Content:** Original product showcases and behind-the-scenes to establish creator identity.
4. **Attribution SOP:** Place `🎥: @creator` in the first 2 caption lines, tag media, never crop watermarks, and state a cooperative dispute policy in bio.
